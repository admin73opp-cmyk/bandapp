Privacy Policy

Ritovo (bandapp.app) · Last updated July 4, 2026

Ritovo is a scheduling and organisation app for bands and music groups. This policy explains what information Ritovo collects, why, and how it's protected. Ritovo is built and operated as an independent app; it is not affiliated with any band-management or streaming service.

Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressAccount creation, sign-in, and transactional emails (invites, password resets)
Name, instrument, bio, phone number (optional)Shown to your bandmates on your profile
Profile / cover photos, band photosDisplayed in your profile, band page, and rehearsal/concert galleries — only if you choose to upload one
Band data you createSongs, set lists, rehearsals, concerts, availability, notes — the core content of the app
Feedback you submitBug reports and feature requests sent from within the app

Ritovo does not collect location data, advertising identifiers, or browsing history, and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

How data is stored and protected

  • All data is stored in a managed Postgres database (Supabase), encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Row-level security ensures you can only see data belonging to bands you are a member of.
  • Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed before storage and never visible to us.
  • Photos are stored in access-controlled cloud storage tied to your band membership.

Who can see your data

Your profile and the band data you create are visible only to other confirmed members of the same band(s) you belong to. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (invites, password resets)

These providers process data strictly on our behalf to operate the app and do not use it for their own purposes.

Your rights

You can update or delete your profile information, photos, and band data at any time from within the app. To request full account deletion (including removal of your account and associated personal data from our systems), email us at the address below and we will process the request within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Ritovo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of Ritovo after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
