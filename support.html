In-app feedback

You can also report a bug or request a feature directly from inside Ritovo — tap the feedback button (bottom-right) on any screen.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in. Try resetting your password from the sign-in screen. If you signed up recently, check your inbox (and spam folder) for a confirmation email.
  • I was invited to a band but didn't get the email. Ask your band admin to resend the invite, or check your spam folder.
  • How do I delete my account? Email us at the address above and we'll process the request within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
